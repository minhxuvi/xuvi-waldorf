# XuViStory

Help Waldorf teachers learn, remember, and tell stories in their own words—then bring them to children without a screen.

## Video Demo

I will choose two unfamiliar stories with similar length, complexity, and intended audience. I will prepare one with XuViStory and the other using the text alone, with equal preparation time.

I will retell both without text or image prompts after the same delay. XuViStory will evaluate both using the same criteria, supported by an independent teacher’s review without knowing which method I used. A separate clip will demonstrate practice with image prompts.

This is an exploratory comparison, not proof of learning effectiveness.

## Evaluation Criteria

- **Waldorf-informed storytelling:** vivid language, coherent narrative, and space for children’s own imagination and interpretation.
- **Story fidelity:** preserve key events, relationships, causes, and culturally meaningful details without requiring word-for-word recall.
- **Age appropriateness:** consider the listeners’ age, developmental needs, language, story length, and emotional intensity.
- **Delivery:** clarity, pacing, pauses, and vocal expression, assessed from the recording rather than the transcript alone.
- **Useful feedback:** identify strengths, missing or altered details, and one practical next step, with excerpts or timestamps. No overall score or ranking.

## Features

- **Story library:** upload DOC/DOCX, PDF, HTML, Markdown, or plain text; review extracted text and read in the app. Keep source and usage-rights information with each story.
- **Audience settings:** choose the listeners’ age, language, context, and a personal practice goal.
- **Story map:** organize a story into editable key scenes, characters, and essential details; keep chapters connected for longer stories.
- **Visual recall:** generate or upload images for key scenes. Choose wet-on-wet watercolor, wax crayon, colored pencil, or other styles. Review images for cultural accuracy and age suitability; teachers can replace them with keywords or their own sketches.
- **Practice modes:** rehearse with image prompts, gradually hide them, or tell freely without prompts. Images support the teacher’s preparation; children do not need to use the app.
- **Recording:** record or upload audio, replay it, and correct the transcript. Label each attempt **With image prompts** or **Without image prompts**, and track text or keyword assistance separately.
- **Feedback and retry:** compare the retelling with the selected story version and story map, distinguish omissions from intentional adaptations, then record another attempt.
- **Practice history:** compare attempts under clearly labeled conditions and revisit stories through an optional seven-day practice rhythm.
- **Privacy and control:** keep stories and recordings private by default; let users export or delete them and explain when data is sent to AI services.
